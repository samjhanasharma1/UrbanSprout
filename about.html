About Us

UrbanSprout is a team of four members dedicated to promoting urban gardening and sustainable living. We help people grow plants in small spaces like balconies, rooftops, and homes.

Our Goal

  • To encourage people to make greener cities and help people confidently grow and care for plants by providing simple,practical and easy-to-follow advice for all experiences levels.
  • To create a supportive community where plant lovers can connect, share their experiences, and learn from each other’s successes and challenges.
  • To promote friendly practices that care for both plants and the planet, while helping people build a stronger connection with nature.
